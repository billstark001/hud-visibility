# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Independent visibility settings for HUD-visible and HUD-hidden (F1) states.
- F3+B hitboxes, F3+G chunk borders, player names, other entity names, text-display names/content, and first-person hands/items.
- Cloth Config screen, Options entry, Fabric Mod Menu integration, and NeoForge config integration.
- English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese translations.
- Fabric and NeoForge targets for Minecraft 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.2, and 26.3.
- Preserve the upstream multi-version build infrastructure and MIT notice.
